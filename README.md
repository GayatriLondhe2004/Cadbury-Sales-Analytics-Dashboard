## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end Sales Analytics Dashboard for Cadbury India
analyzing ₹167.7M in revenue across 10 products,
5 regions, and 4 sales channels (2023–2025).

**Developed as MCA Major Project**
Swayam Siddhi College of Management & Research, Mumbai University
Internship: Upskill Campus & UniConverge Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze product-wise revenue and profitability
- Identify top-performing regions and sales channels
- Track monthly and yearly sales trends
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights

## 🔑 Key Business Insights
- 🏆 **Celebrations Box** = Top revenue product (₹60.4M — 36% of total)
- 🌍 **North India** = Highest contributing region (21.4%)
- 📈 **Q4 festive season** drives peak sales every year
- 💎 **Premium category** = 65% of total revenue
- 🛒 **Modern Trade** = Most efficient sales channel

## 🗄️ SQL Queries Covered
- Revenue by Product, Region, Channel
- Gross Profit Margin by Product
- YoY Growth % (Window Function — LAG)
- Top Performing Region per Year (RANK)
- Festive Season Revenue Analysis
- CTE-based advanced queries
- 19 queries total
